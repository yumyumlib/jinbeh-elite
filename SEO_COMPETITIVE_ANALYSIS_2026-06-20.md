## 1. "best hibachi frisco"

### Who ranks 1-5
1. Yelp -- "TOP 10 BEST Hibachi in Frisco, TX" (multiple variant pages)
2. DallasNav -- "The Absolute Best Hibachi in Frisco [Updated 2026]"
3. TripAdvisor -- "THE 10 BEST Japanese Restaurants in Frisco"
4. Shoji Sushi & Hibachi (theshoji.com) -- direct restaurant site
5. TikTok -- user-generated video results

### Content format of top organic (non-directory) result
DallasNav uses a **listicle with 10 restaurants**, each with: photo, address
with Google Maps link, phone, website link, hours, highlights table, 2-3
paragraph review, and cross-links to related city pages. ~3,000 words.
Embedded Google Maps iframe. Newsletter lead-capture popup. Author bio
with photo. "[Updated 2026]" in the title for freshness signal.

### Key differentiators
- DallasNav: embedded map, structured hours, highlights table per
  restaurant, heavy internal linking to related city/cuisine listicles
- Yelp/TripAdvisor: aggregated reviews, star ratings, photo counts,
  filter/sort UX, user-generated content volume
- Shoji: direct restaurant ranking for its own brand name + "frisco"

### What Jinbeh is missing
- **No dedicated "best hibachi frisco" landing page.** jinbeh.com/frisco
  ranks but is a general location page, not optimized for "best hibachi."
- jinbeh.com/blog/best-hibachi-dallas-tx exists but targets Dallas, not
  Frisco specifically.
- No embedded Google Maps on location pages.
- No structured comparison content (why Jinbeh vs. competitors).
- DallasNav lists Jinbeh as #9 of 10 -- Jinbeh's own content should
  position it as the clear #1 with proof points (since 1988, D Magazine
  award, OpenTable Diners' Choice, 200+ Yelp reviews).

### Recommended actions
- Create `/blog/best-hibachi-frisco-tx` -- a 2,000+ word guide that
  positions Jinbeh first, mentions 4-5 competitors fairly, includes
  an embedded map, comparison table, FAQ section, and links to
  `/frisco/hibachi` and reservation CTAs.
- Add an embedded Google Maps iframe to `/frisco` page.
- Add FAQ schema to `/frisco` targeting "best hibachi in Frisco"
  questions.
