Warm &amp; Family-Oriented (Primary)</strong></p><p>Genuine warmth, care, inclusivity. Greet guests like old friends. Emphasize family and togetherness (“bring the whole family,” “making memories together”).</p><p><strong>2. Spirited &amp; Entertaining</strong></p><p>Celebrate the fun of hibachi. Energetic with humor and excitement (without being childish). Highlight the “dinner and a show” aspect.</p><p><strong>3. Authentic &amp; Respectful</strong></p><p>Honor Japanese culinary tradition. Style &amp; Formatting Rules</strong></h1><h2><strong>Emojis</strong></h2><p>Sparingly on social media (1–2 max, end of sentence). None in formal copy or serious feedback. Allowed: fire emoji for hibachi, sushi emoji for specials. Google Posts: one emoji for attention is acceptable.</p><h2><strong>Exclamation Points</strong></h2><p>Max one per sentence. Let words convey excitement, not punctuation.</p><h2><strong>Hashtags</strong></h2><p>Instagram: 2–3 relevant hashtags (#HibachiFun, #DFWSushi, #FriscoTX). No long strings. No hashtags on Google Posts.</p><h2><strong>Humor</strong></h2><p>Family-friendly, gentle puns okay. Never mock customers. Acceptable: “Our chefs are on fire (literally!)”. Options: Noto Serif JP, Yu Mincho, Garamond, Cambria. Conveys authenticity and refinement.</p><p><strong>Secondary (Body Text)</strong></p><p>Clean sans-serif for legibility. Options: Helvetica Neue, Tahoma, Verdana, Source Sans Pro, Open Sans.</p><p><strong>Accent (Optional)</strong></p><p>Brush script or hand-drawn kanji-style for branded materials only.
